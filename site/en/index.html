Elevation, climbs and kilometre splits 0.5

⛰️ Climbs and elevation profile

  • Under a GPS route: climb, descent and elevation profile for running, walking, hiking, cycling, skiing and other outdoor sports.
  • Every kilometre is marked with a dotted line, with its pace below (min/km, pauses excluded); cycling shows km/h. The last partial kilometre is shown lighter.
  • Heights come from the National Land Survey of Finland's elevation model (ground level, not trees or buildings), not from the phone's GPS. Finland for now.
  • Elevation data downloads per municipality like maps: Download elevation data on the maps page, or the setting Elevation data with maps. Small files, e.g. Kerava 39 kB, Helsinki 0.3 MB. Also in the map search and importable from a file (SHA-256).

❤️ Health Connect and notification

  • Add completed exercise from Health Connect now also finds gym sessions: the watch data (time, heart rate, calories) is added to the day's gym workout, or a new gym workout is created.
  • Also steps without a workout, e.g. from a walking pad: a continuous walk of at least 10 minutes shows in the list, e.g. "4032 steps 10.23–11.05". Pick the sport and the exercise is filled in.
  • One notification for a workout in progress: the activity and elapsed time (gym, stopwatch or GPS run). The rest timer stays separate. Setting in the ☰ menu.
  • Map search: search by municipality, city or region.

Completed exercise from Health Connect 0.4.2

  • Add completed exercise from Health Connect (☰ → Settings, off by default): a home screen button lists the day's workouts not yet in Gievra, e.g. saved by a walking pad, a watch or another app. A tap opens the exercise pre-filled: sport, time, distance, heart rate, calories and steps. Check it and save.
  • Steps, distance and calories come from the app that saved the workout, so steps your phone counted at the same time don't replace them.
  • A saved exercise can now be shared and deleted (⋮ menu).
  • Fixed: with three-button navigation, the end of pages stayed under Android's button bar.

Routes snap to paths, safer map import 0.4.1

🥾 Routes snap to paths

  • GPS easily drifts a few metres sideways. When the area's map is downloaded, the workout's map has Snap route to paths and a 3–10 m slider: drift is corrected onto the path, and the route follows its bends. You see right away how route, distance and pace change; time doesn't.
  • On foot, footpaths, cycle paths and pavements win over car roads; it never snaps to a major road on foot.
  • In the forest and wherever no path is near, the GPS route is kept as it is. A brief brush with a path isn't enough.
  • The original GPS route stays in the .gpx file, so the correction can be changed or removed later; it also works for older GPS runs. Running, walking, Nordic walking, hiking and cycling.

🗺️ Maps from a file

  • Download maps from this page (map search) or anywhere, and import them into the app. A map is recognised by its content (SHA-256), so the file name doesn't matter.
  • Verification shows its progress; the whole of Finland is checked in a few seconds.
  • Older map versions are recognised too. Without a connection: import the maps together with the checksum table.
  • If verification fails: Check online, Import without verifying (marked as unverified) or Skip. An incomplete or broken file is never imported.

Heart rate, calories and steps from your watch 0.3

  • Health Connect (Android): after a workout, Watch data → Health Connect. Your watch app moves the session to Health Connect, and Gievra fetches average and max heart rate, calories and steps.
  • Only this workout, not your whole history: Gievra looks for the session close to the workout's time and asks Is this the one? – add or skip.
  • Works for gym workouts, manually logged activities and GPS runs. You can still type the values by hand.
  • Read only: Gievra writes nothing to Health Connect, and no data is sent online.
  • Also: + Gym and + Activity side by side on the home screen, and steps for running too.

GPS and offline maps in seven countries 0.2.9

  • 📍 GPS mode for running, walking and cycling, or Manual e.g. on a treadmill. GPS is always optional.
  • Bad fixes, impossible speeds and spikes are filtered out during the workout. Standing still doesn't add distance.
  • Recording continues in the background with the screen off, without Google services.
  • The route is shown on a map and saved as a .gpx file next to the workout: an open format that other apps can read.
  • Offline maps in seven countries: Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Iceland by municipality, the United Kingdom by local authority and Spain by province. Download by radius (running and walking 20 km, cycling 100 km, adjustable, also across borders) or pick areas yourself. Maps work without a connection and can be deleted after a trip or updated.

⬇️ Download maps on a computer or straight to your phone

The app downloads maps by itself, but you can also get them here, e.g. over Wi-Fi or on a computer, and move them to your phone.

    1. Download the area map (.pmtiles) and, once, the country's overview map.
    2. Move the files to your phone, e.g. via a synced folder (Seafile, Nextcloud…) or USB.
    3. No connection on the phone: also take the checksum table (kartat-tarkisteet.json, SHA-256 of every map version) and pick it together with the maps when importing, and the app verifies them.
    4. In Gievra: ☰ → Settings → Maps → From file and pick the files.

    Try it and build your own program

    🧪 Demo in your browser

    The app runs right in your browser with four weeks of example workouts: home screen, calendar, workout logging and statistics. Nothing is saved, so feel free to try everything.

    📋 Program builder

    Answer a few questions to build a program: ☰ → Program and goals → Create new program → Save template. You get a .gmd program template you can import into the phone app or send to a friend.

    🔒 Private

    The web version doesn't contact Google or any other service: the app, fonts and emoji come from this site. On your phone, workouts are plain files in a folder you choose.

    What it does

    💪 Gym workouts with one hand

    • Start gym workout: the program day is suggested based on recovery (muscles worked today and yesterday), and a day you've already done is marked
    • big +/– controls, last time and best result, records 🏆
    • a set is saved with one tap: Reps in reserve?
    • warm-up sets filled in and a weight suggestion based on last time
    • How did it feel? 😫 😕 😐 🙂 🤩 – after a hard workout, feedback per exercise
    • rest timer (optional): longer rests for heavy sets, alerts with sound and vibration even with the screen off

    📋 Ready-made programs

    • powerlifting, muscle growth, fat loss, general fitness or just for fun
    • kettlebell programs and bodyweight training without a gym
    • a 10-week powerlifting cycle and deload weeks
    • program check and weekly volume per muscle group
    • you can start from your own FitNotes history
    • .gmd program templates to share; on Android they open straight in Gievra

    🏃 Running and other sports

    • choose Gym, Cardio or Both; switch views on the home screen with one tap
    • add a run with one tap, most-used sports first
    • over 30 sports (plus Other sport, with or without GPS); walking and hiking also record steps
    • running has its own progress view: pace and average speed, kilometers, easy-run pace and heart rate, records
    • optional GPS: route on a map and as a .gpx file, offline maps for Finland, the Nordic countries, the UK and Spain
    • smartwatch data (heart rate, calories, steps) from Health Connect or by hand; Garmin Connect activities can be imported as a CSV file

    📈 Progress and statistics

    • overview for a week, a month, 3 or 6 months and a year
    • all workouts together, or gym and cardio separately: sessions, time, sets, volume, kilometers, heart rate and by sport
    • workouts per week or month, including those imported from FitNotes and Garmin
    • weekly volume per muscle group: planned and done side by side
    • strength progress from estimated maxes and feeling per week

    ⚖️ Body weight (optional)

    • Log morning weight on the home screen when you haven't weighed yourself yet; weigh in whenever you like
    • a comma or a dot both work (82,4 or 82.4)
    • weight trend and BMI in statistics
    • weigh-ins and height are edited in Settings; FitNotes weigh-ins are imported too

    🩹 Recovery and injuries

    • injury mode: 20 body areas, exercises to avoid and alternatives
    • sick or vacation break with an easier return
    • a deload week reminder after a long streak of training
    • 148 exercises, each with a How to do it animation and technique tips

    📁 Your files

    • workouts, settings, program and weigh-ins as plain Markdown files (.md)
    • sync with Nextcloud, Seafile, Syncthing or anything else
    • import from FitNotes, Garmin Connect and .md files; backup as a zip file
    • importing again never duplicates: existing workouts and weigh-ins are kept

    What does the app connect to?

    We checked: the Gievra 0.3 release was installed on an Android emulator, and every network connection the app opened was logged while it was being used (November 2026).

    Setup, program and gym workoutno connections
    GPS run and finding your locationno connections: location and route stay on the phone
    Health Connect (heart rate, calories, steps)no connections: read on the phone, read only
    Maps screen and map downloadsGitHub only (github.com and release-assets.githubusercontent.com; Check online also api.github.com)
    Bug report and backupno connections: Android's share menu, the app you choose does the sending
    In the background and at startupno connections

    When you download maps, GitHub sees your IP address and which municipality packs you download, but not your location, device identifiers or any account. The app has no analytics, ads, crash reporting or Google Play services. Links (source code, support, OpenStreetMap) open in your browser only when you tap them.

    Languages

    EnglishThe whole interface. Exercise and sport names in English; search finds them in both English and Finnish. Files are always stored in the same format, so you can switch language at any time.
    Suomi (Finnish)The default, the whole app. Exercise and sport names in Finnish, English or both.
    Davvisámegiella (North Sámi)Experimental: all texts and sport names. The translations are machine translations checked with a Sámi spell checker, so they may contain mistakes. Exercise names in Finnish or English, exercise instructions in Finnish. Corrections are especially welcome.

    Sámi-inspired colors, open source

    The colors come from the Sámi flag and the decorative band from the trim of the gákti. The greeting is always in North Sámi – Buorre iđit, Buorre beaivi, Buorre eahket – and the date is also shown in North Sámi.
