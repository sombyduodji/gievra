Try it and build your own program

🧪 Demo in your browser

The app runs right in your browser with four weeks of example workouts: home screen, calendar, workout logging and statistics. Nothing is saved, so feel free to try everything.

📋 Program builder

Answer a few questions to build a program: ☰ → Program and goals → Create new program → Save template. You get a .gmd program template you can import into the phone app or send to a friend.

🔒 Private

The web version doesn't contact Google or any other service: the app, fonts and emoji come from this site. On your phone, workouts are plain files in a folder you choose.

What it does

💪 Gym workouts with one hand

  • Start gym workout: the program day is suggested based on recovery (muscles worked today and yesterday), and a day you've already done is marked
  • big +/– controls, last time and best result, records 🏆
  • a set is saved with one tap: Reps in reserve?
  • warm-up sets filled in and a weight suggestion based on last time
  • How did it feel? 😫 😕 😐 🙂 🤩 – after a hard workout, feedback per exercise
  • rest timer (optional): longer rests for heavy sets, alerts with sound and vibration even with the screen off

📋 Ready-made programs

  • powerlifting, muscle growth, fat loss, general fitness or just for fun
  • kettlebell programs and bodyweight training without a gym
  • a 10-week powerlifting cycle and deload weeks
  • program check and weekly volume per muscle group
  • you can start from your own FitNotes history
  • .gmd program templates to share; on Android they open straight in Gievra

🏃 Running and other sports

  • choose Gym, Cardio or Both; switch views on the home screen with one tap
  • add a run with one tap, most-used sports first
  • over 35 sports; walking and hiking also record steps
  • running has its own progress view: run type, pace and average speed, kilometers, easy-run pace and heart rate, records
  • smartwatch data (duration, calories, heart rate) is entered by hand for now; Garmin Connect activities can be imported as a CSV file

📈 Progress and statistics

  • overview for a week, a month, 3 or 6 months and a year
  • all workouts together, or gym and cardio separately: sessions, time, sets, volume, kilometers, heart rate and by sport
  • workouts per week or month, including those imported from FitNotes and Garmin
  • weekly volume per muscle group: planned and done side by side
  • strength progress from estimated maxes and feeling per week

⚖️ Body weight (optional)

  • Log morning weight on the home screen when you haven't weighed yourself yet; weigh in whenever you like
  • a comma or a dot both work (82,4 or 82.4)
  • weight trend and BMI in statistics
  • weigh-ins and height are edited in Settings; FitNotes weigh-ins are imported too

🩹 Recovery and injuries

  • injury mode: 20 body areas, exercises to avoid and alternatives
  • sick or vacation break with an easier return
  • a deload week reminder after a long streak of training
  • 147 exercises, each with a How to do it animation and technique tips

📁 Your files

  • workouts, settings, program and weigh-ins as plain Markdown files (.md)
  • sync with Nextcloud, Seafile, Syncthing or anything else
  • import from FitNotes, Garmin Connect and .md files; backup as a zip file
  • importing again never duplicates: existing workouts and weigh-ins are kept

Languages

EnglishThe whole interface. Exercise and sport names in English; search finds them in both English and Finnish. Files are always stored in the same format, so you can switch language at any time.
Suomi (Finnish)The default, the whole app. Exercise and sport names in Finnish, English or both.
Davvisámegiella (North Sámi)Experimental: all texts and sport names. The translations are machine translations checked with a Sámi spell checker, so they may contain mistakes. Exercise names in Finnish or English, exercise instructions in Finnish. Corrections are especially welcome.

Sámi-inspired colors, open source

The colors come from the Sámi flag and the decorative band from the trim of the gákti. The greeting is always in North Sámi – Buorre iđit, Buorre beaivi, Buorre eahket – and the date is also shown in North Sámi.

Gievra is free software under the GNU GPL v3.0: you may use, study, change and share it. The source code is on GitHub.

Exercise guides, animations and programs are compiled from research and open textbooks, but a professional hasn't reviewed them yet. Check your technique with a coach or physiotherapist before lifting heavy.
